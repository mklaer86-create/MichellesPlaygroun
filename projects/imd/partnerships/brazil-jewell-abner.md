Jewell replied to Michelle's "IMD Details and Teaching Next Steps" email.
Her answers, question by question:

- **Timing:** February or March 2027 both work. Avoid February 5–10
  (Carnival) — people travel or go on church retreats and it's a "crazier
  time."
- **Needs:** Encourage the leaders, teach self-leadership and practical
  ways to lead people, plus emotional intelligence and growing in spiritual
  maturity.
- **Venue:** Yes — the church building in Manaus, and the church in
  Autazes.
- **Teaching times/schedule:** Proposed Manaus as a Saturday (all day) and
  Sunday morning (or Friday night + Saturday all day); Autazes as possibly
  3 evenings during the week. Asked back: how many hours does IMD need for
  the training? Also flagged a decision point — a 1-week trip (Manaus
  weekend, then Autazes weeknights) vs. 2 separate weekends (one per city,
  with days off in between) — and asked which IMD would prefer.
- **Lodging & transportation — Manaus:** Tropical Executive Hotel is an
  option. Transportation depends on team size — Uber is available, they
  have one vehicle, but renting a 7-seater/van may be more practical for a
  larger team.
- **Lodging & transportation — Autazes:** Ellen Palace Hotel or Tupinambá
  Hotel — they'll reserve once dates are confirmed. Will need to rent a
  vehicle; the trip is 2 ferries plus a drive, about 5–7 hours total.
- **Translation & printing:** Yes, they can help coordinate locally.
- **Translators:** Need two. Abner could translate, but Jewell expects to
  be distracted with the new baby, so better to have someone else. They can
  reach out to Kelly or others once dates are confirmed.
